## Summary

We logged 48 ideas from April to August. 29 passed Gate 1, and 11 have passed Gate 2: 5 went into Q3 and 6 are ready for Q4. At the final Q4 portfolio review, the cut line fell at 32 team-sprints, so the 6 highest-scoring ideas are ready for planning. The rest stay ranked for next quarter.

## Findings

- **Most ideas stop at Gate 1, as intended.** 19 of 48 ideas were redirected to Home Team backlogs as Discretionary work, or declined, before any design work.
- **Medium-sized ideas score best.** Five of the six ideas above the cut line are size M. Their value is close to the larger ideas', for less than half the effort.
- **Online first report of injury just missed Q4.** It has the highest time criticality, but at size L it would take the plan to 40 team-sprints. It ranks first for Q1 2027.
- **Two large ideas score low,** the return-to-work planner and medical bill review rules. Both could be split into smaller projects with clearer early value.

## Actions

- **Claims Experience Product Manager:** prepare online first report of injury for Q1 2027, and check whether a smaller first release could fit Q4 as Discretionary work.
- **Product Managers for the low-scoring L ideas:** propose splits before the October portfolio review.
- **Program Manager:** take the 6 Gate 2 ideas into capacity planning.
