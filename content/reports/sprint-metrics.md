## Summary

Teams met their sprint goal in most of the last six sprints. Velocity is steady, which makes the next quarter's plan easier to trust.

## Findings

- Sprint-goal hit rate is highest for teams that refine two sprints ahead.
- One team's velocity dipped while it covered on-call for an incident.

## Actions

- Share the two-sprints-ahead refinement practice in the next Scrum Master community meeting.
