## What it is

Product Operations helps Pecos Mutual keep its platforms running and change them for the better. All of our work is one of two kinds:

- **Run** keeps today's platforms healthy. When something breaks, we fix it through Incident Management.
- **Change** makes something new or better, either as a **strategic project** or as smaller **Discretionary** work for a Home Team.

## Why it matters to you

Whatever your job, you'll meet the model at a few predictable moments: when you ask for something, when something breaks, when you're asked for your expertise, and when we show you what we've built.
