The operating model only works because many people outside Product Operations take part in it. Business Executives sponsor ideas and sign off plans, engineers build and go on call, claims and service teams tell us what they need and test what we build, and security, finance and service desk teams all have a part to play. Start with the guide for the work you do, or jump straight to a how-to.
