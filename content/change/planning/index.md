Planning is the second stage of the Change lifecycle. Projects arrive scoped, with high-level designs, and prioritized. Resource Management and planning practices then break each one into goals and milestones for delivery within about 3 months.

## What it is

Planning runs on a **fixed quarterly cycle**. Each quarter is six two-week sprints. In the four weeks before a quarter starts, three things happen in order:

| When | Activity | Led by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 weeks before | [Resource and capacity planning](/change/planning/capacity-planning/): how many team-sprints each Home Team has, the Run allowance, and who joins which project team | Program Manager |
| 3 to 2 weeks before | [Goals and milestones](/change/planning/goals-and-milestones/): a milestone plan for each project | Project Manager |
| Last week before | [Quarterly Planning](/change/planning/quarterly-planning/): a one-day session where teams confirm their plans and Business Executives sign them off | Program Manager |

The final portfolio prioritization of the quarter happens just before capacity planning. It decides which projects pass Gate 2 and come into this cycle.

## Why it matters

- **Plans fit the capacity we actually have.** Capacity planning takes the Run allowance out first, so incidents and Platform Health work don't eat the plan.
- **Three months is short enough to be honest.** Milestones in the next quarter can be planned with real people and real dates. Anything longer than a quarter is split.
- **One sign-off moment.** Business Executives see every project, every dependency and every trade-off at once, at Quarterly Planning.

## Who's involved

- **Program Manager:** leads capacity planning and runs Quarterly Planning.
- **Project Manager:** builds the milestone plan for each strategic project and owns its risks.
- **Product Owner:** checks that milestones match the backlog and turns the first milestone into backlog items ready for refinement.
- **Product Manager:** keeps goals tied to the outcome in the business case.
- **Business Executives:** sign off the quarterly plan.

## Home Teams in Planning

Home Teams don't need a milestone plan for their Discretionary work. Capacity planning does reserve their Discretionary share, and their Product Owners bring the main Discretionary items to Quarterly Planning so that dependencies show up.

## How it connects

Planning takes its input from [Discovery](/change/discovery/) and hands each project team a milestone plan to start [Delivery](/change/delivery/) on the first day of the quarter.
