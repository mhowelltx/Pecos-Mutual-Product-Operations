## When to use

Run Quarterly Planning once a quarter, in the last week before the quarter starts, after every project has a milestone plan.

## Roles

- **Program Manager:** leads. You run the agenda, keep time, own the dependency board and record decisions.
- **Project Managers:** present each project's milestone plan and its risks.
- **Product Owners:** present Home Team Discretionary priorities and confirm that each milestone plan is realistic for their team.
- **Product Managers:** answer scope and value questions, and agree trade-offs.
- **Business Executives:** join the opening and the sign-off, and decide any trade-off the teams can't settle.
- Scrum Masters are welcome, and often help facilitate the team breakouts.

## Before you start

- Have the capacity plan, every milestone plan and the ranked portfolio list shared two working days ahead.
- Set up a dependency board, physical or online, with one column per sprint and one row per team.
- Book the Business Executives for the first 30 minutes and the last hour.

## Agenda

| Time | Session |
| --- | --- |
| 09:00 | **Opening** (30 min). Business Executives restate the business priorities. The Program Manager walks through the ranked list and the capacity plan. |
| 09:30 | **Plan walk-throughs** (2 hours). Each Project Manager presents goals, milestones, capacity fit and top risks. Each takes about 10 minutes, plus questions. |
| 11:30 | **Home Team priorities** (30 min). Product Owners share their main Discretionary items for the quarter. |
| 13:00 | **Team breakouts** (2 hours). Teams adjust their plans and post dependencies on the board. Each dependency needs agreement from the team that has to provide it. |
| 15:00 | **Risks and trade-offs** (1 hour). Go through the open risks, unresolved dependencies and unstaffed projects, and decide what to cut, move or accept. |
| 16:00 | **Confidence vote and sign-off** (1 hour). Each team votes 1 to 5 on its own plan. Any vote under 3 is discussed and the plan fixed before sign-off. Business Executives then sign off the quarterly plan. |

## Steps

1. **Open with the why.** Tie the day to the business priorities and the ranked list, so trade-offs have a reference point.
2. **Walk through every plan.** Keep to time, and park detailed questions for the breakouts.
3. **Make dependencies visible.** Every cross-team need goes on the board with a sprint and both teams' agreement. Mark any dependency without agreement in red.
4. **Resolve the red items.** In the risks and trade-offs session, decide each red dependency and each unstaffed project: fix it, move it, cut it, or accept the risk with an owner.
5. **Take a confidence vote.** Fix any plan with a vote under 3 before sign-off. Don't sign off a plan the team doesn't believe in.
6. **Sign off.** Business Executives approve the quarterly plan as a whole.
7. **Publish** the signed-off plan, the dependency board and the decisions within one working day.

## Outputs

- A signed-off quarterly plan: every project's milestones, plus each Home Team's Discretionary priorities.
- A dependency board with an agreed date for every cross-team dependency.
- A risk log with owners.
- Recorded decisions on cuts, moves and unstaffed projects.

## Pitfalls

- **Status reports instead of planning.** The day is for making decisions. If a plan walk-through turns into an update, move it to the breakouts.
- **Dependencies agreed by one side.** A dependency only counts once the team that must provide it agrees.
- **Signing off everything.** If the confidence votes are low, cutting scope now costs less than missing milestones later.
