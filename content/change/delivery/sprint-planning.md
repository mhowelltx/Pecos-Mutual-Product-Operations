## When to use

At the start of every sprint, once the backlog has been refined.

## Roles

- **Product Owner:** brings the ordered backlog and proposes the sprint goal.
- **Scrum Master:** facilitates and keeps the session to its timebox.
- **Developers:** size the work and commit to the sprint backlog.

## Before you start

- Refine the top of the backlog so items are ready.
- Confirm team capacity for the sprint, including leave and on-call.

## Steps

1. Restate the product goal and propose a sprint goal.
2. Pull ready items from the top of the backlog until capacity is reached.
3. Break each item into tasks the team can finish within the sprint.
4. Agree the sprint goal and commit to the sprint backlog.

## Outputs

- A sprint goal everyone can say in one sentence.
- A sprint backlog sized to capacity.

## Pitfalls

- Planning to 100% capacity leaves no room for Run work and incidents.
