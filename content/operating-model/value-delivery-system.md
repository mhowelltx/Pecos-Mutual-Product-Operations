## What it is

The value delivery system is how Pecos Mutual turns business needs into working products and keeps them running. It has two workstreams.

- **Run** is run-the-business work. Teams handle it through **Incident Management** and **Platform Health**.
- **Change** is **strategic projects** delivered by cross-functional project teams, plus **Discretionary** work delivered by static **Home Teams**.

## Why it matters

Naming the workstream tells everyone who owns the work, how it is funded and which playbook to follow.

## How it connects

Change work moves through three stages:

1. **Discovery:** ideation, estimation and prioritization. Business Executives work with Product Managers.
2. **Planning:** resource management, then goals and milestones for delivery within about 3 months.
3. **Delivery:** the project team practices Scrum to deliver the project.
