You keep one strategic project on its path. You turn a prioritized project into goals and milestones, manage its risks and dependencies, and keep everyone's view of when it will land honest.

## Responsibilities

- Write the milestone plan for your project each quarter: goals, milestones with "done when", dependencies and risks.
- Review the proposed project team with the Program Manager, and flag missing skills.
- Present your plan at Quarterly Planning, and get every dependency agreed by the team that provides it.
- Update the milestone forecast at every sprint review, as on track, at risk or late.
- Chase dependencies and escalate risks. You don't assign tasks inside a sprint; that's the team's job.

## Where you show up

**Change: Planning.** This is your home. You lead [goals and milestones](/change/planning/goals-and-milestones/), review staffing in [capacity planning](/change/planning/capacity-planning/), and present at [Quarterly Planning](/change/planning/quarterly-planning/).

**Change: Delivery.** You join the start of [sprint planning](/change/delivery/sprint-planning/) to link the sprint goal to the next milestone. At the [sprint review](/change/delivery/sprint-review-retro/), you update the forecast.

**Run.** When an incident pulls people off your project team, you work with the Product Owner on the effect on the milestone plan.

## Your top tasks

1. [Set goals and milestones](/change/planning/goals-and-milestones/) for your project each quarter, using the [milestone plan template](/templates/milestone-plan/).
2. Present your plan and agree dependencies at [Quarterly Planning](/change/planning/quarterly-planning/).
3. Update the milestone forecast at every [sprint review](/change/delivery/sprint-review-retro/).
4. Join [sprint planning](/change/delivery/sprint-planning/) to confirm what the sprint contributes.

## Who you work with most

- The **Product Owner** and **Scrum Master** of your project team.
- The **Program Manager**, on capacity and on dependencies with other projects.
- The **Product Manager**, who owns the outcome your goals serve.
