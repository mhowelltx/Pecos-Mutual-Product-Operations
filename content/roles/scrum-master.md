## Responsibilities

- Coach the project team in Scrum.
- Facilitate sprint events and keep them to their timebox.
- Remove impediments, or escalate them to the right owner.

## Where you show up

- **Change, Delivery:** every sprint event.
- **Run, Incident Management:** you help the team plan around incident work and run post-incident reviews.

## Your top tasks

1. Facilitate sprint planning.
2. Track and clear impediments from the daily scrum.
3. Run the sprint review and retrospective.
