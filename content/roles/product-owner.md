You decide what your team builds next, and you're accountable for the value each sprint delivers. You own one ordered backlog: for a project team, the strategic project's backlog; for a Home Team, its Discretionary and Run backlog.

## Responsibilities

- Order the backlog, so the most valuable work is always at the top.
- Write and agree acceptance criteria, and get two sprints of work ready through refinement.
- Propose the sprint goal, and decide sprint scope changes when Run work interrupts.
- Accept or reject work at the sprint review, against the Definition of Done.
- On a Home Team: agree the Run allowance and the Discretionary share in capacity planning, and order reliability actions from incidents and Platform Health reviews.

## Where you show up

**Change: Delivery.** This is your home. You lead the content of sprint planning, refinement and the sprint review.

**Change: Planning.** You confirm your team's capacity, check the milestone plan is realistic, and break the first milestone into backlog items. At [Quarterly Planning](/change/planning/quarterly-planning/), you present your Home Team's Discretionary priorities.

**Change: Discovery.** You join the [high-level design review](/change/discovery/high-level-design-review/) and [T-shirt estimation](/change/discovery/t-shirt-estimation/) for ideas that touch your platforms.

**Run.** Actions from post-incident reviews and Platform Health reviews land in your backlog. The error budget rule tells you when reliability work goes first.

## Your top tasks

1. [Refine the backlog](/change/delivery/backlog-refinement/) mid-sprint, every sprint.
2. [Run a sprint planning session](/change/delivery/sprint-planning/) with your Scrum Master.
3. [Run the sprint review](/change/delivery/sprint-review-retro/) and change the backlog based on the feedback.
4. [Set goals and milestones](/change/planning/goals-and-milestones/) with your Project Manager each quarter.

## Who you work with most

- The **Scrum Master** of your team, as co-lead of every sprint event.
- The **Product Manager**, who owns the outcome your backlog serves.
- The **Project Manager**, on strategic projects, who owns the milestone plan your sprints deliver.
