Change
: Strategic projects delivered by cross-functional project teams, plus Discretionary work delivered by static Home Teams.

Discretionary work
: Smaller, ongoing change delivered by a static Home Team instead of a project team.

Home Team
: A static team that owns an area and delivers its Discretionary work.

Run
: Run-the-business work done by teams through Incident Management and Platform Health.
