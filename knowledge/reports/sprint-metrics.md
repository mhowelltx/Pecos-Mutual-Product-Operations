# Sprint metrics roll-up

> All figures in this report are fictional demo data. As of 2026-09-25. Period: Q3 2026, sprints 1 to 6. Source: Pecos Mutual sprint tracker export (fictional).

**Headline:** Most teams hit their sprint goal. **Key figure:** 83% Hit their sprint goal.

## Summary

Our three reporting teams met their sprint goal in 15 of 18 Q3 sprints, an 83% hit rate. Velocity stayed steady for Claims Platform and Policy Platform. Agent Experience was steady until sprint 6, when an incident and its follow-up took two people for most of the sprint.

## Findings

- **Claims Platform met every sprint goal,** at an average velocity of 42 points. The team refines two sprints ahead, so it rarely starts a sprint with unclear items.
- **Policy Platform missed one sprint goal,** in sprint 3, when a dependency on another team arrived late.
- **Agent Experience missed two sprint goals,** in sprints 2 and 6. Its sprint 6 velocity fell to 20 points after the Sep 15 Agent Portal incident.
- **Velocity measures a team against itself.** We use it for planning within each team, not to compare teams.

## Actions

- **Scrum Masters:** share Claims Platform's two-sprints-ahead refinement practice at the next Scrum Master community meeting.
- **Agent Experience Product Owner:** plan Q4 sprint 1 at a lower velocity while the incident follow-up actions finish.
- **Policy Platform Scrum Master:** confirm cross-team dependency dates at sprint planning, as the sprint planning playbook asks.
