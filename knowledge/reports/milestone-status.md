# Program milestone status

> All figures in this report are fictional demo data. As of 2026-09-11. Period: Q3 2026 strategic projects, after sprint 5 of 6. Source: Pecos Mutual milestone plans, updated at the sprint 5 reviews (fictional).

**Headline:** Two projects are running late. **Key figure:** 11 of 16 On time milestones met or forecast.

## Summary

After sprint 5, 11 of our 16 Q3 milestones are done or forecast in their planned sprint. Three projects are on track. Two are late: agent commission statements by one sprint, and the payroll reporting API by up to two. Two milestones will move into Q4.

## Findings

- **Claims status, the provider identity upgrade and the underwriting rules refresh are on track,** with every milestone done or forecast on time.
- **Agent commission statements will finish one sprint late.** Switching off paper statements moves into Q4 sprint 1, after a late start in sprint 2.
- **The payroll reporting API has slipped each milestone.** Integration with large employers' payroll systems has taken longer than its design review assumed. "Ten employers live" is now forecast for Q4 sprint 2.
- **The two late projects both depend on teams that were busy with incidents** in August and September.

## Actions

- **Project Manager, payroll reporting API:** re-plan the last milestone with the Product Owner, and bring the Q4 effect to Quarterly Planning as a dependency.
- **Program Manager:** reserve Q4 sprint 1 and 2 capacity for the two milestones that move into Q4, before new projects start.
- **Project Managers:** update forecasts again at the sprint 6 reviews on Sep 25.
