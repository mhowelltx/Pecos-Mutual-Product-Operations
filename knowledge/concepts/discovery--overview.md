# Discovery

Discovery is the first stage of the Change lifecycle for strategic projects. Business Executives work with Product Managers on high-level ideation, estimation and prioritization. Discovery ends when a project is ready for planning, or when we decide not to do it.

## What it is

Discovery answers three questions before anyone commits a team:

1. **Is it worth doing?** A one-page business case says what problem we solve, for whom, and how we'll know it worked.
2. **Roughly how big is it?** A high-level design and a T-shirt estimate give a size we can plan with.
3. **Is it more important than everything else?** Portfolio prioritization ranks it against the other ideas competing for the same capacity.

Discovery is for strategic projects only. Discretionary work goes straight into a Home Team's backlog after a light intake. See [Run, Change and Discretionary work](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/operating-model/run-change-and-discretionary-work/) to check which path an idea takes.

## The two gates

An idea passes two decisions on its way through Discovery.

- **Gate 1: Idea accepted.** At the intake review, a Product Manager and a Business Executive sponsor agree that the business case is worth more work. The idea then gets a high-level design and an estimate.
- **Gate 2: Ready for planning.** The project has a reviewed high-level design, a T-shirt size of L or smaller, and a portfolio ranking above the cut line for the coming quarter. It moves to [Planning](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/planning/).

An idea that stops at either gate is recorded with the reason, so it can come back later if things change.

## Who's involved

- **Product Manager:** leads Discovery. You write the business case, run the design review and present the project at portfolio prioritization.
- **Program Manager:** runs T-shirt estimation and brings the capacity forecast that sets the portfolio cut line.
- **Business Executives:** sponsor ideas, confirm the business value and agree the final ranking.
- **Product Owners** and senior engineers from the affected Home Teams join the design review and estimation.

## Typical timing

Most ideas take 3 to 6 weeks from intake to Gate 2. Intake reviews run every two weeks, and portfolio prioritization runs monthly, so an idea is ready in time for the next Quarterly Planning session.

## How it connects

Discovery feeds Planning. The outputs of Gate 2 are the inputs to [resource and capacity planning](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/planning/capacity-planning/) and to [goals and milestones](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/planning/goals-and-milestones/).
