# Product Manager

You decide what is worth building and why. You work with Business Executives to shape ideas into strategic projects, and you're accountable for the outcomes and the health of the platforms in your area.

## Responsibilities

- Work with Business Executive sponsors to write one-page business cases with measurable outcomes.
- Lead the high-level design review, and present your ideas at portfolio prioritization.
- Keep project goals tied to the outcome in the business case through Planning and Delivery.
- Own the health of your platforms, and lead their monthly Platform Health review.
- During incidents, judge customer impact and approve customer-facing messages.

## Where you show up

**Change: Discovery.** This is your home. You lead [idea intake](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/discovery/idea-intake/), the [high-level design review](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/discovery/high-level-design-review/) and [portfolio prioritization](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/discovery/portfolio-prioritization/).

**Change: Planning.** You check goals in each milestone plan, and you answer scope and value questions at [Quarterly Planning](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/planning/quarterly-planning/).

**Change: Delivery.** You join sprint reviews to see the work and give feedback, and join refinement when items need a scope decision.

**Run.** You lead the [monthly platform health review](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/run/platform-health/monthly-health-review/) for your platforms. During [incidents](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/run/incidents/triage-and-declare/), you confirm the impact and approve customer messages, and you join post-incident reviews.

## Your top tasks

1. [Take in an idea with a one-page business case](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/discovery/idea-intake/), using the [template](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/templates/one-page-business-case/).
2. [Run a high-level design review](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/discovery/high-level-design-review/) for each accepted idea.
3. [Prioritize the portfolio](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/change/discovery/portfolio-prioritization/) every month.
4. [Run the monthly platform health review](https://pecos-mutual-product-ops.pages.dev/run/platform-health/monthly-health-review/) for your platforms.

## Who you work with most

- **Business Executives**, who sponsor ideas and sign off the plan.
- The **Program Manager**, on intake, estimation and the portfolio ranking.
- The **Product Owners** of the teams that build and run your products.
